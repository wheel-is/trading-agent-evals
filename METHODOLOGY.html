We ran the task-specific research rubric against every selected saved research answer. We report relevance, raw correctness, critical-gated correctness, uncertainty and all-pass separately.

Automated review completed; financial validation incomplete. Candidate grades are actual review outputs, not final expert-certified financial labels. The release gate remains active. This is not the earlier four-check task-fulfilment proxy.

Rubric

The original 157-check research rubric maps all126 original task checks, with research-only adaptations and explicit common source/time/claim checks. Local weights are5for critical,3for major and1for minor checks; they are design choices, not validated industry constants. The P02–P11 adaptations are post-hoc. This run does not claim preregistration or approved domain calibration.

Three independent reviews

Three fresh-context AI judges reviewed full anonymized prompts and responses in separate isolated workstreams. Product self mentions were replaced by [product]; original hashes and the identity map remain in the export. Judges did not read the prior completion labels, identity map or peer votes. Different review instructions emphasize mandate/evidence, contradiction/omission and reproducibility/contract. Blinding effectiveness was not experimentally measured, and URLs/style may still reveal identity.

All three use the inherited configured model. Its exact identifier was not exposed to the judges; no distinct-family jury or calibrated independence is claimed. They generated5,181 explicit votes over1,727 case-criterion decisions, each with a concise rationale and exact answer quote or whole-answer omission basis. Evidence quotes were checked against the blinded source text; UTF-8 spans and hashes are stored. Invalid support is rejected, not silently accepted.

Unanimous supported votes become candidate labels. For noncritical criteria, two matching labels plus unknown can become a candidate label. Any pass/fail split, critical non-unanimity or unsupported evidence remains unknown pending expert adjudication. Agreement is descriptive and does not establish truth. Full peer votes remain in candidate-grades.json.

Evidence and deterministic verification

Directly inspectable output requirements and conditional policies can be judged against the actual prompt. A formula or toy graph does not establish an actual ranking; an incomplete screen cannot establish exhaustive absence. Honest permitted unresolved answers can satisfy relevance, while substantive outcome correctness stays unknown. Empty baskets do not receive vacuous eligibility, ranking or equal-weight passes.

The evaluator independently retrieved dated BLS payroll/CPI records and reconstructed several P09 methods using Decimal arithmetic: monthly payroll/CPI acceleration; payroll3-month versus12-month averages and compounded inflation; published-precision zero/carry conventions; and full-precision CPI year/year changes. These facts support matching checks only. Different declared defensible methods can yield different classifications and ETF allocations. No target majority is used as a truth oracle.

Other full historical task source packs are incomplete. Fed/ALFRED fetch failures are unknown, not proof of fabrication. A URL, citation or self-reported calculation is not independently verified just because it exists. The source audit lists actual checks, gaps and calculation inputs. Deterministic verifier tests cover dates, lower-bound/member thresholds, lobbying growth, all four ETF maps, exact SoFi trigger/fee boundaries, and independent iterative/linear PageRank fixtures. Synthetic fixture success is not real-world source validation.

Scores and critical gates

For an axis, let P,F,U be passed,failed,unknown weight and T=P+F+U. Lower=100P/T; upper=100(P+U)/T; decided coverage=100(P+F)/T. Unknown stays in the denominator. Empty axes are null. Stored values retain full precision; display uses one decimal.

A failed correctness dealbreaker sets the task’s gated correctness lower and upper to0. An unknown dealbreaker sets its lower to0 while preserving the raw upper bound. Raw correctness remains a separate diagnostic. Required all-pass is true only when every required applicable check passes, false when any fails, otherwise unknown. The report’s axis-specific all-pass controls use the same rule within that axis; full quality all-pass remains in each case ledger. Benchmark all-pass additionally depends on verified scope and timing.

Common-task aggregates average each task equally within each configuration. Mostly single-trial captures do not establish reliability or confidence intervals. Planned trials, observation snapshots and technical retries are different units. Chart ordering is by lower bound and then upper bound for display; overlapping intervals are not a winner claim. No single combined relevance/correctness index is part of the original design.

What remains before financial release

The original strict release schema requires approved rubric/applicability, independently validated complete historical source/reference packs, passing deterministic checks and calibrated semantic routes or qualified human adjudication. The rubric remains draft; complete source coverage and expert calibration are absent. Official financial grades therefore remain unknown, as the designed release gate requires. The separate candidate scores expose what the actual AI rubric review concluded without relabeling them as released financial accuracy.

No qualified-expert labels or adjudications were supplied. The design calls for at least220 independently expert-labeled criteria spanning all eleven templates, including100 known critical failures, third-review conflict resolution and separate held-out validation. Its proposed automatic-release thresholds are at least95% label agreement and at most1% observed critical false-pass rate. These thresholds have not been demonstrated. Calibration status and release status are explicit.
