Methodology

This retrospective evaluation applies the original research rubric without changing its 1/3/5 weights, critical flags or task denominators. Each case receives three independently prompted, anonymized AI reviews. Deterministic arithmetic and primary-source checks are used where evidence is available. Pass/fail splits and critical non-unanimity remain unknown. Scores use the existing scoring engine; no relevance/correctness composite is invented.

Means give each common task equal weight. Unresolved results remain in the denominator, submitted failures remain attempted cases, and P01 pilot and Jango paper treatments stay separate. Read the exact procedure, source coverage and release limits.
